# MiroFish Run #4 — Agent Roster

**As of Day 0 (January 2027)**

**All named individuals and organizations in this roster are fictional simulation actors, except where explicitly noted as real. Agent positions update only in response to OBSERVED events. No agent position may be updated based on SEED facts or INFERRED reasoning alone.**

---

## Design Principle

This roster prioritizes **decision power** over commentary volume. The agents most represented are:
- Workflow blockers (can stop or clear a workflow)
- Adversarial competitors (benefit if GenLayer loses)
- Commercial capture intermediaries (may adopt GenLayer technically but own the relationship)
- Forced beneficiaries (create pressure on decision-makers)
- High-friction skeptics (whose standards are difficult to satisfy)

Agents whose role is general market commentary without decision impact have been removed or deprioritized.
